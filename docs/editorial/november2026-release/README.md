# GrantTap Journal: November 18, 2026

Prepared and published on **November 18, 2026**:
[GrantTap 1.0.2 is on the App Store: what’s new on your phone and your computer](https://granttap.com/blog/granttap-1-0-2-app-store-whats-new).

Facts follow the shipped product on that day:

- GrantTap 1.0.2 for iPhone and iPad (build 295) on the App Store. Apple's public
  lookup for app 6809739366 reports version 1.0.2, first release November 13 and
  current-version release November 18; its release notes match the 1.0.2 entry
  in the version history.
- GrantTap MCP 0.8.195 on npm; each helper feature names the version (0.8.188–0.8.195)
  whose release commit introduced it.
- GrantTap 1.0.1 for Mac (build 291) in App Review since November 15, available by
  TestFlight invitation.
- The next iPhone update (builds 296–305) is prepared but not submitted. The story
  describes its features as arriving after Apple approves it and gives it no
  version number or date.

nine sections. The counts are recorded in
