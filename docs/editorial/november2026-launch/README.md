# GrantTap Journal: November 14–16, 2026

Prepared and published on **November 16, 2026**. November 14 and 15 are editorial
slots; their pages show the actual publication date beside the slot. Facts follow
the shipped product on that day: GrantTap 1.0.1 for iPhone and iPad on the App
Store (build 289), GrantTap 1.0.1 for Mac in App Review (build 291), and the
granttap-mcp versions published on npm, each named where a feature depends on it.

Every article has English and Russian versions with at least 2,000 body words
each (opening and section paragraphs; headings, captions, the setup box, sources
and the closing note are excluded), and three original illustrations. The counts are
recorded in [calendar-validation.json](calendar-validation.json) and checked by
`app/blog/tests/november-launch.test.tsx`.

## Images

The nine WebP images in [public/blog/november2026-launch](../../../public/blog/november2026-launch)
were generated with the Grok CLI's image generation tool, run headless with one
prompt per image, and converted with `cwebp`. [image-prompts.json](image-prompts.json)
records each prompt, the shared style, the command, and SHA-256 hashes of the
original and the published file. They are conceptual illustrations, labelled as
such in both languages; they are not product screenshots or measurements, and
contain no text, logos or people.
